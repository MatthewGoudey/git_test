# A Field Guide to Humanity

A briefing on the human species and its knowledge, written for an intelligent reader from elsewhere who knows nothing about Earth. It was produced from the prompt in [`../prompts/human-knowledge-for-an-alien.md`](../prompts/human-knowledge-for-an-alien.md).

**Focus:** concrete, well-established knowledge that shapes human life: economics, politics, law, technology, health. Every important claim is labeled by how well supported it is. Religion, spirituality, and ideologies are covered in depth **as human phenomena**: their history, practices, and effects. Their supernatural or value claims are never presented as facts.

## Claim labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **[Established]** | Strong evidence and broad expert agreement (assumed when no label is shown) |
| **[Refining]** | Well supported, but details still under study |
| **[Contested]** | Experts disagree, or evidence is weak or mixed |
| **[Belief/Value]** | A supernatural or moral/political claim that evidence cannot settle |

*Figures describe the mid-2020s and are rounded. This guide was written from general knowledge without live data lookups, so check specific numbers against primary sources (UN, World Bank, IMF, WHO, Pew Research, IPCC) before relying on them.*
